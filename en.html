Flip Runner

A 3D runner where you guide a tiny fairy along the ceiling and walls of a spinning tunnel

FAQ

How do I play?
You run forward automatically. Tap the left of the screen to jump to the left wall, the right to jump to the right wall, and the middle to flip to the ceiling. The tunnel spins so the wall you chose is under your feet. Hit an obstacle on your current wall and the run is over. Tap Run Again on the result screen to start the next run right away.

What are close calls and combos?
Switch walls right before an obstacle to get a Close Call bonus. Chain them together for a combo and bigger bonuses.

The spinning makes me feel dizzy
Turn on Settings → Controls → Reduce Motion. The tunnel stops bending, twisting and shaking, and the spin becomes shorter. If you feel unwell, stop playing and take a break.

Can I swipe instead of tapping?
Choose Swipe in Settings → Controls: swipe left or right for the side walls, and up or down for the ceiling. On iPad you can also use the keyboard (← → ↑ / Space) or a game controller.

The screen rotates while I'm playing
The app locks the screen orientation during a run. On iPad it may not stay locked in Split View or a window, so please play in full screen.

What is Continue?
Once per run, after a crash you can keep going from where you crashed. It's available after watching a video ad to the end. If you buy Remove Ads, you can continue without an ad.

What is the Daily Course?
A mode where everyone gets the same course for the day. Your best score for that day is saved. The day changes according to your device's calendar.

How do I unlock fairy colors?
Reach 250 m for Bronze, 500 m for Silver, 900 m for Gold and 1500 m for Rainbow. Each medal unlocks a new fairy color you can pick on the title screen.

Can I change the design?
Switch between Soft and Cyber any time in Settings → Design.

It stutters or my device gets warm
The app draws a 3D scene, so animation may be less smooth on older devices or in Low Power Mode. Closing other apps may help.

There's no sound
Sounds don't play when the Ring/Silent switch is set to silent. You can change the volume in Settings → Sound & Haptics. The app can play alongside music apps.

Where are my records (best score, Daily Course, medals) saved?
Only on your device. They move to a new device when you restore from an iPhone backup, and are erased if you delete the app. You can also start over with Settings → Reset Records.

How do I remove ads?
Buy Remove Ads in Settings (a one-time purchase). After switching devices, use Restore Purchase on the same screen. Full-screen ads appear only when you start the next run (never while you are running).

Reporting an inappropriate ad
Tap the ⓘ (AdChoices) mark in the corner of the ad to report it to Google, which serves the ads. You can also tell us when you saw it and what it showed via "Contact" below. In the App, Settings → Report an Inappropriate Ad opens this page.
